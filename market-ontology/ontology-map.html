Centralization & harmonization structure

The core cross-market finding, as a picture: Germany is deliberately bilateral; Netherlands and Japan both centralized but via different institutional routes (cooperative datahub vs. statute-mandated paid intermediary); ENTSO-E sits above the EU members as a TSO-level layer that only recently (Jan 2025) started reaching down into retail data-access rules.
