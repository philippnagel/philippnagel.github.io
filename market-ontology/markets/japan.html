japan

japan

Aug 16, 20264 min read

Overview

Japan’s architecture is built around the 10 General Transmission & Distribution Operators (一般送配電事業者, TDOs), each owning meters and running its own MDMS/HES stack, feeding a single shared national aggregation layer (集約システム, built/operated by GDBL) and a single certified data intermediary (電力データ管理協会) that mediates all third-party access. Consumer-facing device communication uses ECHONET Lite rather than the internationally dominant DLMS/COSEM, making Japan a structural outlier among large industrialized markets.

Standards body & governance

ECHONET Consortium owns ECHONET Lite (ISO/IEC 14543-4-3) and runs SMA/AIF certification. Wi-SUN Alliance owns the 920MHz radio layer (IEEE 802.15.4g-based); TTC (Telecommunication Technology Committee) adopted Wi-SUN enhanced HAN as national standard JJ-300.10. The 10 TDOs coordinate through TDGC (送配電網協議会) for common specs (e.g. the 特定計量システム external-connection standard, common across all 10 TDOs). 電力データ管理協会 is a METI-certified body (認定協会 under Electricity Business Act Art.37) governing consent and third-party data access nationally.

Protocol stack

Field/radio layer: Wi-SUN 920MHz multi-hop (IPv6) for Route A (meter → TDO MDMS, billing-grade); Wi-SUN enhanced HAN (ratified May 2024) for the IoT-Route (cross-utility gas/water/EV/PV co-metering). Consumer interface layer: ECHONET Lite object model — global standard equivalent would be DLMS/COSEM (IEC 62056), which ~65% of non-China smart meters worldwide use; Japan, China, and the US are the major exceptions. Data format layer: proprietary per-TDO / 電力データ管理協会 APIs rather than a standardized interchange format (contrast DLMS+OBIS codes, NEM12/13 in Australia, GBCS in UK, EDIFACT-energy in Germany). Retailer-facing data leg for the public-server side runs OCCTO EDI (separately confirmed in vault, EDI Standard Ver.3A).

Settlement & switching

Meter → TDO MDMS (Route A) → per-TDO HES (real-time) → MDMS (reading store/MDM + asset mgmt/AM + network mgmt/NM) → 集約システム (single national aggregation layer, 3-year retention, 30-min push from each TDO) → 電力データ管理協会 (consent/access management) → 利用会員 (utilization members, tiered by data type). Supplier switching and settlement run over the logical C-Route interface between TDO and retailer. Settlement granularity is 30-minute (Gen-1 and Gen-2 standard), with Gen-2 adding 5-minute collection for ≥10% of LV meters — later than Australia’s national move to 5-minute settlement (Oct 2021).

Datahub / centralization

Two-tier centralization: 集約システム is the single national data-aggregation layer across all 10 TDOs (public-private hybrid, GDBL-operated), and 電力データ管理協会 is the single certified national access/consent intermediary sitting above it. This is the most centralized model among the markets in this ontology so far — contrast Germany’s explicit rejection of any central intermediary (germany).

Notable strengths / failure modes

Strengths: near-100% Gen-1 rollout (fast vs UK/DE/US); IoT-Route is genuinely ahead internationally — no other country uses the electricity meter network as a shared data backbone for gas/water at national scale; 10-TDO common spec suppresses procurement fragmentation costs seen in the US’s 50-state model. Failure modes: consumer direct data access is structurally absent — unique among compared markets, since access always routes through a paying service-provider intermediary (basis of a known opt-out fee controversy, ¥44,000 from April 2028); concentration risk — TEPCO PG + Chubu + Kansai + NTT Data effectively control the only legal national data intermediary (GDBL); Gen-2’s 5-minute granularity arrives ~5 years after Australia’s equivalent move; ECHONET Lite isolation forces international meter vendors (Landis+Gyr, Itron, Sagemcom) to build Japan-specific variants.

Open questions

  • No message-format-level detail yet on 電力データ管理協会/OCCTO EDI structure comparable to how EDIFACT UTILMD/MSCONS are documented for Germany — would need a dedicated pass through vault’s Japan Sources folder for full protocol grammar.
  • Whether/how ECHONET Lite’s international isolation is likely to change (Mitsubishi Electric × Landis+Gyr MoU, Jan 2026, flagged in source as partial bridge — status unclear).
  • Full B-Route/IoT-Route distinction and 特定計量システム (sub-metering) protocol detail exists in vault (Japan Smart Metering/Sources/*) but wasn’t pulled into this note in phase 1 — worth a follow-up pass since it’s some of the most detailed material in the vault.
